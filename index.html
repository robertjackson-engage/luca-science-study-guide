MASS · WEIGHT · INERTIA

Mass: amount of matter. Inertia: resistance to a change in motion. More mass → more inertia.

Weight: force of gravity on an object (W = mg). Changes with gravity (Moon, mountaintop). Mass and inertia never change with location.

Doubling volume is not doubling mass. Pouring water out lowers mass, inertia, and weight together.

NEWTON'S LAWS

1st (inertia): no net force → velocity stays the same.

2nd: F = ma. Same force, more mass → less acceleration.

3rd: forces come in equal and opposite pairs. Bug vs truck, bat vs ball: the forces are equal; the lighter one accelerates more.

3 ways to accelerate: speed up, slow down, change direction. A car turning at constant speed is accelerating.

VECTOR OR SCALAR?

Vectors (size + direction): weight, force, gravity, friction, tension, normal, applied, net force, displacement, velocity, acceleration, terminal velocity.

Scalars (size only): mass, speed, average speed.

NA: inertia, vector, scalar, equilibrium, free fall.
